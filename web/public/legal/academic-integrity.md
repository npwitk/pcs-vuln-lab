# Academic Integrity Policy

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

This Academic Integrity Policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service). Because Peanut Cheat Sheet Marketplace sits close to a sensitive line for a student platform, we've given this its own dedicated page rather than burying it in the general Terms.

> **Important Notice:** This platform provides supplementary study materials. Users are responsible for ensuring their use of purchased content complies with their own institution's academic integrity policies.

## 1. What This Platform Is For

Cheat sheets on this platform are meant as **study aids** — condensed reference material to help you review and prepare — not substitutes for learning, and not a way to bring unauthorized material into an exam.

## 2. What We Expect From You

- Using purchased content to cheat or plagiarize violates academic integrity, both ours and your institution's
- You are responsible for understanding and following your own course policies
- Instructors may prohibit the use of cheat sheets — including ones bought here — in their courses, and it's on you to know and respect that
- Content containing actual exam questions or answers from official university examinations is not permitted on this platform at all (see our [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use))

## 3. What We Don't Take Responsibility For

- We are not responsible for academic consequences of content misuse
- We do not guarantee that any content will help you achieve specific academic outcomes
- We do not review content for accuracy against any specific course's current curriculum

## 4. Reporting a Concern

If you believe a specific cheat sheet crosses the line — for example, it appears to contain actual exam material rather than a study aid — please report it through the [Support page](/support) or email [hey@npwitk.com](mailto:hey@npwitk.com). We review and remove content that violates this policy.

## 5. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [hey@npwitk.com](mailto:hey@npwitk.com) or through the [Support page](/support).
