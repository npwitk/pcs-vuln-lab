# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

This Privacy Policy is intended to align with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) ("PDPA").

Welcome to the Peanut Cheat Sheet Marketplace ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

By accessing or using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our service.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us when you:

- Register for an account using Google OAuth authentication
- Make purchases on our platform
- Upload cheat sheets as a seller
- Contact our support team
- Participate in surveys or promotions

This information may include:

- **Email address** (restricted to @g.siit.tu.ac.th domain)
- **Name** as provided by your Google account
- **Profile picture** from your Google account
- **Student identification information**
- **Payment information** (processed securely through our payment provider)

### 1.2 Analytics and Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information about your device and how you interact with our platform. This includes:

- **Session data:** Information about your login sessions, session duration, and authentication status
- **Click data:** Records of buttons clicked, links followed, and features accessed to understand user behavior and improve our platform
- **Search data:** Search queries, search results, and filters used to help us improve search functionality and content recommendations
- **Browser information:** Browser type, version, and settings
- **Device information:** Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
- **IP address:** Your internet protocol address for security and analytics purposes
- **Usage patterns:** Pages visited, time spent on pages, navigation paths, and feature usage
- **Download history:** Records of cheat sheets downloaded and timestamps

- **Referral tracking:** When you use the Share button on a cheat sheet, we generate a link tagged with an identifier and record when that link is opened, including whether the visitor is logged in. This helps us understand how content gets discovered and shared.
- **Transfer codes:** If you generate a one-time transfer code to download a purchased file on another device, we log the IP address used to redeem it and enforce a cooldown period after use to prevent abuse.

### 1.3 Transaction Information

- Purchase history and transaction records
- Payment method information (PromptPay)
- Billing information
- Order details and receipts

### 1.4 Content You Provide

- Cheat sheets uploaded by sellers (including PDF files and metadata)
- Reviews and ratings of cheat sheets
- Support tickets and communications
- Feedback and survey responses

### 2.1 Service Delivery

- **Session analytics:** To monitor active users, detect unauthorized access, and improve authentication security
- **Click analytics:** To understand which features are most used, identify usability issues, and optimize user interface design

- To analyze usage patterns and optimize platform performance

- To conduct research and development for new features

### 2.3 Communication

- To send transactional emails (purchase confirmations, download links)
- To notify you about important platform updates
- To respond to support requests and inquiries
- To send administrative information about your account

### 2.4 Security and Legal Compliance

- To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
- To enforce our Terms of Service and protect intellectual property
- To comply with legal obligations and respond to legal requests
- To maintain the security and integrity of our platform

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS protocols and at rest in our databases
- **Access controls:** Strict access controls limit who can view or process your data
- **Session management:** Secure session storage using PostgreSQL with automatic expiration
- **Authentication:** JWT-based authentication with 7-day token expiry
- **Watermarking:** PDF files are watermarked with user information to prevent unauthorized sharing
- **Rate limiting:** Protection against automated attacks and abuse
- **Regular security audits:** Periodic review of security practices and vulnerabilities

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 With Service Providers

- **Google:** For OAuth authentication services
- **Stripe:** For processing PromptPay payments. For orders below Stripe's minimum or when Stripe is unavailable, we generate a static PromptPay QR code directly and an administrator manually verifies the transfer
- **Hosting providers:** For cloud infrastructure and database services
- **Analytics services:** For aggregated usage analytics (data is anonymized where possible)
- **Telegram:** Order and payment notifications are sent to our administrators via Telegram, which may include a buyer's name and email address
- **Resend:** We use Resend to send transactional emails (purchase receipts, payroll reports, and promotional codes)

### 4.2 With Content Creators

When you purchase a cheat sheet, the seller may see:

- Your name (for watermarking and transaction records)
- Purchase date and transaction information
- Number of downloads (for analytics purposes)

### 4.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe such action is necessary to:

- Comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests
- Protect our rights, property, or safety
- Investigate potential violations of our Terms of Service
- Prevent fraud or security threats

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### 5.1 Access and Correction

- View your account information and purchase history
- Update your profile information through your account settings
- Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

### 5.2 Account Deactivation

- You may deactivate your account at any time through the Support page
- Deactivation prevents future logins but preserves purchase history and transaction records
- Contact support to reactivate a deactivated account

### 5.3 Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting support. Please note:

- Transaction records may be retained for legal and accounting purposes
- Content you uploaded as a seller may remain available if already purchased by others
- Analytics data may be retained in anonymized form

### 5.4 Opt-Out Options

- You may opt out of non-essential communications
- You cannot opt out of transactional or administrative emails
