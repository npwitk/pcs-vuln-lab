# Cookie Policy

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

This Cookie Policy is part of our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and explains how Peanut Cheat Sheet Marketplace uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

## 1. What Cookies We Use

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

- Maintain your login session
- Remember your preferences and settings (such as light/dark theme)
- Analyze site traffic and usage patterns
- Track click events and search behavior for analytics
- Improve platform performance and user experience

## 2. Types of Cookies

- **Essential cookies:** Required for the platform to function — for example, keeping you logged in and remembering items in your cart. The platform will not work correctly without these.
- **Preference cookies:** Remember choices like your theme (light/dark/system) and language.
- **Analytics cookies:** Help us understand how the platform is used, so we can improve it — see our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) for what analytics data we collect and why.

## 3. Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Disabling essential cookies will affect platform functionality — you may not be able to stay logged in or complete purchases.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services we use (such as Stripe, for payment processing) may set their own cookies when their functionality is embedded on our platform. We do not control these cookies directly — refer to those providers' own privacy and cookie policies for details.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
