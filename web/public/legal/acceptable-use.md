# Acceptable Use Policy

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

This Acceptable Use Policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and sets out, in one place, what you may not upload, share, or do on Peanut Cheat Sheet Marketplace.

## 1. Prohibited Content

You may not upload, share, or distribute content that:

- Violates copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights
- Contains exam questions or answers from official university examinations
- Promotes academic dishonesty or cheating (see our [Academic Integrity Policy](/legal/academic-integrity))
- Contains malicious code, viruses, or harmful software
- Includes offensive, defamatory, or inappropriate material
- Violates any applicable laws or regulations
- Contains personal information of others without consent

## 2. Prohibited Use of Purchased Content

Purchased cheat sheets are licensed to you personally, for educational use. You may **not**:

- Share, distribute, or resell purchased cheat sheets
- Remove or modify watermarks from PDF files
- Upload purchased content to file-sharing sites or public platforms
- Use automated tools to scrape or download content
- Create derivative works for commercial purposes
- Reverse engineer or attempt to extract protected content
- Use content for purposes other than personal education

## 3. Platform Abuse

Separately from content rules, you may not:

- Attempt to circumvent security measures, rate limits, or access controls
- Use automated tools (bots, scrapers) against the platform
- Attempt to gain unauthorized access to other users' accounts or data
- Generate or misuse transfer codes, discount codes, or other platform features as a way to share paid content beyond your own personal use

## 4. Consequences

Violating this policy may result in content removal, loss of seller privileges, account suspension, or account termination, depending on severity — see the Account Registration and Security section of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) for the general grounds on which we may act.

## 5. Reporting a Violation

If you come across content or behavior that violates this policy, please report it through the [Support page](/support) or email [hey@npwitk.com](mailto:hey@npwitk.com).

## 6. Contact
