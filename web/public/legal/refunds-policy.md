# Refunds Policy

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

This Refunds Policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and explains when and how refunds are available for purchases made on Peanut Cheat Sheet Marketplace.

## 1. No Refunds, by Default

> **No Refunds:** All sales are final. Due to the digital nature of our products and immediate access upon purchase, we do not offer refunds, exchanges, or cancellations once a purchase is completed.

## 2. Exceptions

Exceptions may be made in the following cases:

- Technical issues prevent you from downloading purchased content
- Content is significantly different from its description
- Duplicate charges due to payment processing errors
- Content violates our policies and has been removed

To request a refund under these circumstances, contact support within 7 days of purchase with proof of the issue.

## 3. How to Request a Refund

- Submit a support ticket through your account, or email [hey@npwitk.com](mailto:hey@npwitk.com)
- Include your order ID and a description of the issue
- We aim to respond to refund requests within 30 days

## 4. Payment Disputes and Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge directly with your bank or card issuer (a "chargeback") instead of contacting us first, we reserve the right to immediately suspend your account and revoke access to any content associated with the disputed transaction pending resolution. Repeated or fraudulent chargebacks may result in permanent account termination.

We'd much rather resolve a payment issue directly — please reach out to support before initiating a chargeback with your bank, so we have a chance to fix it.

## 5. Transaction Records

- All transactions are recorded and maintained for accounting purposes
- You can view your purchase history in your account
- Transaction records are kept for a minimum of 7 years

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [hey@npwitk.com](mailto:hey@npwitk.com) or through the [Support page](/support).
